▶ Shorts Wall — random + loop

No login • No API key • Paste a channel → 9 random Shorts playing together on loop

Starts muted (browsers block unmuted autoplay). Click 🔊 or tap any video for sound.
Ready. Paste a channel above and hit Load Random.
How it works (no key, no login):
• The page reads the channel's public data only: RSS feed + /shorts tab (via a CORS proxy) + free Piped/Invidious mirrors.
• That gives roughly the latest ~30–60 Shorts. Shuffle picks 9 at random from that pool and each iframe uses loop=1&playlist=VIDEO_ID so everything loops forever.
• Why max 12 players? 25 simultaneous YouTube players stutter/crash on most laptops & phones. 9 is smooth and still looks like a wall. Use ?channel=@handle&count=9&autoplay=1 to share a wall.
• If a channel can't be resolved, open youtube.com/@handle → Share channel → copy the /channel/UC… link and paste that — it always works without a key.
• Random = random from the recent pool, not from all 1000+ uploads (that would need an API key, which you skipped — good tradeoff).
